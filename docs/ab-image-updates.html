LibrePod OS · OTA updates · design

A/B image updates

CI builds one signed image per release. The device writes it into its spare slot by day, boots it at night under boot counting, and keeps it only if the health gate passes. Otherwise the old version comes back by itself.

The headline

Nothing an update does can brick the device

The running slot and its boot file are never written. A new version gets two counted boots. If its health gate never passes (it fails, panics or hangs and the watchdog resets it), the old boot entry wins again with nobody at the device.

What the updater does

  • By day: vacuum, then acquire
  • At night: snapshot k3s, update --offline, reboot
  • After boot, pending means rolled back: restore and report
  • Unsigned or tampered releases never become bootable

What the spike measured

  • 341 MiB per release (no k3s yet)
  • 35 s download at 20 MB/s
  • 29 s from reboot to blessed
  • Health failure, panic, freeze: all fell back unaided
